# Brand assets

The CoCoCo wordmark is sourced from https://www.wearecococo.com/images/icons/cococo_logo_flexible.svg. The oversized outline is derived from its first C, following the supplied April 2024 CoCoCo style guide. These brand assets remain CoCoCo brand/trademark material; the component library's MIT license does not relicense them.

Sofia Sans Regular/Bold and Sofia Sans Condensed Bold Latin and Latin Extended subsets are sourced from the official website's `/fonts/` directory. They are distributed under the SIL Open Font License in OFL.txt (source: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/sofiasans/OFL.txt).

Fonts and artwork are hosted locally. The documentation makes no external font requests. Brand styling is scoped to the docs shell; component and example themes remain configurable.
